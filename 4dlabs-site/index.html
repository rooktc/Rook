Three core tech modules

Visual–tactile fusion · ViTam

Vision fused with touch, so demonstrations carry contact forces and material response — not just pixels.

Cross-embodiment mapping

Universal adaptation across robot arms, humanoids and quadrupeds — one dataset serves many bodies.

AXON world-model engine

High-physical-consistency world models in a calibrate–scale–simulate loop.

Data quality & verifiability

Automated QA plus human spot-checks close the quality loop, and rights are registered on-chain at the moment of capture — every episode ships with clean provenance and a verifiable quality score.

FAQ

Common questions

How is my privacy protected when I contribute?

Faces, screens, documents and voices are automatically de-identified during upload, before episodes are stored or made available for review or training. You can review your episodes and request deletion in the app; details are in our Data Licensing Terms.

What do I earn for contributing?

Quality-scored uploads earn rewards: 3D spatial scans and their derived applications settle in tokens, and capture missions earn contribution points that unlock alpha perks, hardware priority and early access. Rewards are tied to data quality, not raw volume.

What device do I need?

Any recent iPhone or Android phone works for capture missions and 3D spatial scanning. Ego Suite, EgoBio and DexUMI hardware are optional upgrades for advanced capture tracks.

What kind of tasks should I capture?

Everyday manipulation: cooking, tidying, assembling, repairing, sorting. Guided missions in the app tell you exactly what the network needs most right now.

How do companies license 4Dlabs data?

Through dataset licences per vertical, or custom capture campaigns with exclusivity options. Every delivery includes spec sheets, QA reports and clear usage terms.

Is there a token?

Yes — spatial-scan uploads and derived applications settle in tokens, and data rights are registered on-chain at capture. Full token mechanics ship with the whitepaper; capture-mission points and their conversion path will be documented there too.
