4D Fusion Data Engine

One pipeline from a phone clip to a robot-ready dataset

The engine fuses egocentric video, hand pose, depth and scene geometry into time-aligned 4D episodes — captured in the real world, processed automatically, and delivered in training-ready formats.

  • Capture anywhere. Phones, DexUmi hardware and Ego Suite glasses feed one ingestion pipeline.
  • Automatic 4D reconstruction. Scene geometry, hand–object contact and action segments are extracted without manual labelling.
  • Quality-gated output. Every episode is scored, de-identified and versioned before it reaches a training set.
▶ Asset slot — 15–30s demo loop：採集 → 上傳 → 訓練 → 機器人執行

Three core tech modules

4D scene reconstruction

Rebuilds metric geometry and object motion from monocular capture, so every clip becomes a spatial, replayable scene.

Hand–object interaction capture

Fine-grained hand pose and contact states — the action labels robot manipulation policies actually train on.

Cross-embodiment retargeting

Maps human demonstrations onto different robot bodies, so one dataset serves many hardware platforms.

FAQ

Common questions

How is my privacy protected when I contribute?

Faces, screens, documents and voices are automatically de-identified during upload, before episodes are stored or made available for review or training. You can review your episodes and request deletion in the app; details are in our Data Licensing Terms.

What do I earn for contributing?

Quality-scored episodes earn contribution points, which unlock alpha perks, hardware priority and early access to new capture tracks. Rewards are tied to data quality, not raw volume.

What device do I need?

Any recent iPhone or Android phone works for EgoDo missions. DexUmi hardware and Ego Suite glasses are optional upgrades for advanced capture tracks.

What kind of tasks should I capture?

Everyday manipulation: cooking, tidying, assembling, repairing, sorting. Guided missions in the app tell you exactly what the network needs most right now.

How do companies license 4Dlabs data?

Through dataset licences per vertical, or custom capture campaigns with exclusivity options. Every delivery includes spec sheets, QA reports and clear usage terms.

Is there a token?

Contribution today earns points and early access. If we ever add an on-chain component, we will announce it with full documentation before anything changes.
